Greenhouse monitoring

Repository (personal copy) ↗ Team original

Problem

The partner team collected sensor data from a greenhouse (temperature, soil and air humidity, water volume), but processed and analyzed everything by hand in spreadsheets. The process was slow, error-prone and made it hard to notice quickly when a condition left the ideal range for the crop.

Solution

A website that receives the files generated by the sensors, stores the readings in a database and processes them automatically, showing charts per variable, a data table, averages, a date filter and alerts when values go beyond defined limits.

  • Information architecture and screen prototyping in Figma.
  • Interfaces with the sensor charts and an easy-to-read presentation of the data.
  • Helped with the alert system based on predefined limits.

Problem

The client had no real-time access to rain, wind, humidity, temperature and pressure data, which made it hard to anticipate floods and landslides. Themis

AI-assisted legal precedent analysis

Repository (personal copy) ↗ Flutter app Back-end Team original
Partner company
Xertica
My role
Dev Team, mobile app
Period
Mar to May 2026
Status
Completed

Problem

Lawyers and judges must ground petitions and rulings on precedents, but rulings are spread across many courts and come in large volume. Finding the ones that really apply means reading whole decisions with no objective measure of similarity to the case, which takes time and raises the risk of relying on an inadequate or outdated precedent.

Solution

A mobile app connected to a Python API that analyzes the petition (PDF or case description) with natural language processing, queries an indexed precedent database and returns related rulings with a similarity percentage, an applicability rating, a case summary and how each precedent relates to the case. In lawyer mode, the AI also drafts the initial petition.

My contributions

I was the team member with the most commits in the app repository.

  • Login and sign-up screens, app routes and the petition upload integration.
  • Track 1 (lawyer mode): petition generation with a court filter (STF, STJ, TST, TRF, TRT) and asynchronous polling integration with the API.
  • Result screen with the petition in Markdown, precedents with relevance badge and similarity, and a bottom sheet to edit and regenerate.
  • Petition summary and history, draft ruling and PDF export.
  • JWT authentication with the FastAPI API and a final round of testing and fixes.

CHATin

AI study coach for the ENEM exam

Repository (personal copy) ↗ Front-end Back-end Team original
Partner company
GSW
My role
Product Owner
Period
Sep to Nov 2026
Status
In progress, sprint 2 of 3 done

Problem

Students preparing for ENEM, Brazil's university entrance exam, struggle to know their level in each subject, to decide what to study next in a huge syllabus and to notice their own progress. Tutoring is expensive, feedback on mistakes comes late and the lack of visible progress makes students drop their plan. For the partner, AI usage must be measured and capped, and the system must stay useful even when the AI is down.

Solution

A web app where the student talks freely with an AI coach. The system detects the ENEM subject and topic, generates summaries saved to a private library, creates auto-graded quizzes, tracks progress per topic and adjusts difficulty. It also has a guided textbook, study paths, web source search with citations and gamification (XP, day streaks and a leaderboard by nickname).

My contributions

  • Gathered and documented the client's pain points (checking, planning and controlling study) and the product vision from GSW's challenge.
  • Wrote the 18 functional and 9 non-functional requirements, including the AI cost and resilience constraints.
  • Built the Product Backlog with 20 user stories, with priority, estimates, acceptance criteria and a split into 3 sprints.
  • Defined the DoR and DoD and keep the README and sprint reports up to date for the partner reviews.
  • Set up the initial repository structure (front and back submodules) and helped with visual fixes on the progress screen.
